NovaTech Solutions
Employee Handbook — Fictional Demonstration Dataset
Effective date: 1 January 2026
This company and all policies are fictional and provided only for IntelliChat demonstrations.

1. Working hours
The standard working week is Monday to Friday, 40 hours per week. Normal office hours are
9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m., including a one-hour unpaid lunch break. Teams observe core collaboration
hours from 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. Employees may agree flexible start and finish times with their
manager, provided service coverage and weekly hours are maintained. Overtime requires written
manager approval before the additional work begins.

2. Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accrued at two days
per completed month. New starters receive a prorated allowance. Submit annual leave requests
through the HR portal at least seven calendar days before the intended absence. The manager
approves requests based on staffing requirements. Up to five unused days may be carried into
the next year and must be used by 31 March. Public holidays do not reduce annual leave balances.

3. Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Notify the line manager before
10:00 a.m. on the first day of sickness, or as soon as reasonably possible in an emergency.
A medical certificate is required after more than two consecutive working days of absence.
Sick leave does not carry forward to the next year. Health records are handled confidentially
by HR and are accessible only to authorized personnel.

4. Remote and hybrid work
Eligible employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval.
The default hybrid schedule includes at least three days in the assigned office each week.
Managers may require on-site attendance for workshops, customer meetings, or team events.
Remote work must be performed within the employee's country of employment. Working from
another country is not permitted without prior written approval from HR and Legal, because
tax, immigration, insurance, and data-transfer requirements must be assessed.
Employees must remain reachable during core collaboration hours and use the company VPN.
A private workspace and reliable internet connection are required. Remote work arrangements
are reviewed quarterly. Roles requiring physical equipment may not be eligible.

5. Password security
Passwords must contain at least 14 characters, including uppercase and lowercase letters,
a number, and a symbol. Use unique passwords for each work account and store them in the
approved password manager. Multi-factor authentication is required for email, VPN, payroll,
and source-code access. Never share passwords or approve unexpected authentication requests.
Change a password immediately if compromise is suspected. Report suspicious login prompts
to the security team through the internal incident portal.

6. Data security
Classify information as public, internal, confidential, or restricted before sharing it.
Confidential and restricted information must remain in approved encrypted company systems.
Do not upload client data, employee records, or source code to unapproved external AI tools.
Lock your workstation whenever unattended. Use company-managed devices for business work.
Report suspected data exposure to the security team within one hour of discovery.
Only share files with named recipients who need them for their work; public links are prohibited
for confidential data. Follow the retention schedule and securely delete expired records.

7. Business travel and travel expenses
Business travel requires written manager approval before booking. Book economy-class flights
and standard rooms through the approved travel portal. Personal upgrades are not reimbursable.
Local public transport and reasonable taxi fares for business journeys are reimbursable with
receipts. Meal reimbursement is capped at 60 USD per day during approved overnight travel.
Alcohol, entertainment unrelated to business, and personal purchases are excluded.
For international travel, obtain the necessary travel approval and check visa requirements.

8. Expense reimbursement
Submit expense claims within 30 calendar days of the expense date through the finance portal.
Every claim must include an itemized receipt, a business purpose, and the relevant project code.
The manager reviews the claim before Finance performs compliance checks.
Approved expenses are reimbursed within 10 business days after Finance approval.
Late claims require an exception approved by the Finance manager. Duplicate claims and
personal expenses submitted as business costs may lead to disciplinary action.

9. Employee training
Each employee has an annual professional development budget of 1,200 USD and five paid
learning days. Eligible activities include role-relevant courses, certifications, workshops,
and technical conferences. Obtain manager approval before enrolling or making a payment.
Discuss learning objectives during quarterly development conversations. Share a short summary
of useful learning with the team after completion. Mandatory security and code-of-conduct
training must be completed within 30 days of joining and refreshed annually.

10. Code of conduct
Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect. Discrimination, harassment, bullying,
and retaliation are prohibited. Disclose potential conflicts of interest to HR before taking
decisions that may be affected by a personal relationship or financial interest.
Protect confidential information even after leaving the company. Report misconduct to HR
or the confidential ethics channel. Reports are investigated fairly and in confidence.
Gifts exceeding 50 USD must be declared to the compliance team before acceptance.

11. Company leadership
The Chief Executive Officer is Priya Raman. Priya Raman joined NovaTech Solutions on
15 March 2021 as Chief Operating Officer and became CEO on 1 July 2024.
The employee handbook does not specify company revenue, share price, or future financial forecasts.
